index-pack cost and multi-pack-index usage during backfill
=========================================================

Extracted from each cell's backfill.event.json. The raw traces remain on the
run box because they are hundreds of megabytes per cell.

index_pack_total_s = sum of t_abs for every process whose cmd_name is index-pack.
midx_loads         = count of Trace2 data events with category=midx and
                     key ending in load/num_packs.

Cells C and D ran detached, so their backfill processes are outside the
harness-owned trace. "not observable" does not mean no work was performed.

cell  name           index_pack_total_s  processes  midx_loads  num_packs  backfill_s
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A     midx-on        319.298             33         34          102        375
B     midx-off       827.534             33          0          none       899
C     maint-now      not observable      --         --          --         1552
D     timer          not observable      --         --          --         1294
E     maint-daily    849.066             35          5          138,139    1415
F     repack-first   259.502             35         40          102,103     336

Interpretation:

- A versus B reproduces the multi-pack-index effect on this box.
- E's index-pack total is close to B because E writes its midx after fetch.
- F writes the midx first and its index-pack total is in the same fast group
  as A.
