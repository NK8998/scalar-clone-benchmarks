TASK ORDERING: why E gets no benefit and F does
===============================================

Line numbers below refer to each cell's backfill.perf.txt retained on the run
box.

--- E: maintenance run --schedule=daily ---

Fetch runs first. The multi-pack-index tasks start only after the fetch and its
index-pack children have finished.
